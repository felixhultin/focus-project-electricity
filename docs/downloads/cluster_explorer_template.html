Clustering settings and interpretation
Cluster labels are numeric identifiers, not validated sense names. A changing proportion can reflect sampling, OCR, genre, or definition language as well as semantic change. Years with n=0 have no sampled usages; this does not establish absence in the corpus. TKR and LB use the stored year, without treating January 1 placeholders as precise event dates.
