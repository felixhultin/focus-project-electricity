How these associations are calculated

The source is the available Mats target-usage exports. The notice above the graph identifies whether the selected dataset uses every valid dated usage or a capped yearly sample. Every corpus is analysed separately. Even uncapped exports contain target contexts rather than every sentence in the original corpus.

The resource selector restricts both measures and source excerpts to one newspaper (Kubhist/Kubhist2), document collection (TKR), or author identified by the source URL (Litteraturbanken). Newspaper identities come from source filenames, grouped across years. Word2Vec is trained independently within the selected resource. In uncapped analyses, every eligible usage from that resource contributes. Small subsets can have no eligible annual points or insufficient context for a model.

Co-occurrence share counts a word at most once per target usage within ten word tokens on either side of the selected occurrence. The denominator is all analysed usages of that term in that year. Candidate words need at least two supporting usages. Common Swedish and historical function words are omitted from candidate labels.

Embedding similarity is cosine similarity between the target and a neighbour in a Gensim skip-gram Word2Vec model. For each displayed year, the model is trained on all Mats target contexts in the same corpus from that year and the two years on either side. Contexts contain up to 30 word tokens on each side of the selected occurrence, within a 2,000-character limit per side. The selected target form is normalized to its lemma; neighbouring forms are lowercased without lemmatization. The model uses 100 dimensions, a five-token training window, minimum frequency five, five negative samples, 15 epochs, and seed 42.

A training window needs at least 50 contexts and 3,000 tokens. A target absent from the trained vocabulary has no embedding points for that year. Similarity scores come from independently trained time windows; small samples, changing genres, historical spelling, and OCR can affect the neighbours. Dots are descriptive model outputs, not validated meanings or full-corpus association estimates.
